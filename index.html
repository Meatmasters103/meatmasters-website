Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day delivery? +
Yes! Order before 6PM and we'll deliver fresh to your doorstep the same day. We cover most neighborhoods in the city. Delivery charges are $4.99, or FREE on orders above $30.
Is all your meat Jhatka certified? +
Absolutely. All our chicken, goat, mutton, and other meats are 100% Jhatka Market certified. We follow strict hygiene and processing standards to ensure you get the safest, most trustworthy meat.
Can I choose live fish and have it cleaned? +
Yes! We have live fish tanks with Tilapia, Catfish, Blue Crab, Crawfish, and more. You can select your fish from the tank and we'll clean, cut, and pack it fresh right in front of you — or deliver it cleaned to your door.
What are your store hours? +
We're open every day from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM. For deliveries, place your order before 6PM. You can also WhatsApp us anytime to pre-schedule your order.
How can I place a bulk or catering order? +
We love bulk orders! For weddings, events, or catering requirements, please call us directly at +1 (555) 123-4567 or WhatsApp us with the details. We offer special rates for orders above 20kg.
Do you offer custom cuts? +
Of course! We can do custom cuts, boneless preparation, minced meat, marination, and other special preparations on request. Just let us know your preferences when placing your order.
